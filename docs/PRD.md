BET Project Vision
BET is a personal betting intelligence platform focused on answering a single question: 'Which bets actually work for me?' Rather than predicting games, BET collects historical and future wagers, measures performance, and identifies profitable patterns.
Primary Goal
Analyze the user’s complete betting history to determine which betting behaviors, teams, sports, odds ranges, and wager types generate positive ROI.
Key Questions BET Should Answer
Which teams are most profitable?
Which sports are most profitable?
Are parlays helping or hurting?
Do favorites outperform underdogs?
What odds ranges generate the best ROI?
Which bet types should be avoided?
Should stake sizes be increased or decreased?
What would have happened if only specific strategies were followed?
Core Philosophy
BET is not a sportsbook and not an automated betting bot. It is an analytics and decision-support system that helps a recreational bettor understand personal betting performance and discover repeatable edges.
